Mexican Tacos Recipe


Ingredients:

  • Soft tortillas
  • 300g chicken (or beef)
  • 1 onion (sliced)
  • 1 capsicum (sliced)
  • 2 tbsp taco seasoning
  • Oil & salt
  • Fresh coriander

Method:

  1. Heat oil and fry onion and capsicum.
  2. Add chicken and taco seasoning.
  3. Cook until chicken is tender.
  4. Fill tortillas and garnish with coriander.

Cheese Pizza Recipe


Ingredients:

  • Pizza dough
  • Tomato sauce
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Oregano

Method:

  1. Roll the dough.
  2. Spread tomato sauce.
  3. Add cheese and herbs.
  4. Bake at 200°C for 15 minutes.

White Sauce Pasta Recipe


Ingredients:

  • Pasta
  • Butter
  • Garlic
  • Milk
  • Cheese
  • Black pepper

Method:

  1. Boil pasta.
  2. Heat butter and garlic.
  3. Add milk and cheese.
  4. Mix pasta and sauce.
